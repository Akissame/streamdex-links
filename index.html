Good to know

Questions, answered.

A few details about how StreamDex works, how your information is handled and what you can do with your library.

Need more detail? The privacy section below explains what stays on your device and which external services support StreamDex.
Is StreamDex free?
Yes. StreamDex is free to download and use, with advertising in some areas of the app. There is no subscription required to maintain your library, track what you watch or use its core features.
Do I need to create an account?
No. You can start using StreamDex without signing up or creating a password. Your library, watch history, ratings, achievements and statistics are stored locally on your device.
Does StreamDex work without an internet connection?
Your saved library and locally stored profile remain on your device, so you can still access information already saved there. Searching the catalogue, refreshing title details and checking current streaming availability require an internet connection.
How do XP, levels and achievements work?
You earn experience by tracking your viewing activity and completing in-app achievements. Your accumulated XP contributes to your level and rank, giving you another way to follow your progress as a viewer.
Can I move my library to another device?
Yes. Export your profile from the app to create a .sdx file, then import that file on your other device. The export is initiated by you; StreamDex does not automatically upload your profile to a cloud account.
How do premiere and new episode reminders work?
Notifications are scheduled locally on your device. StreamDex needs an internet connection to refresh release information; once the relevant data is available and notifications are configured, your device can deliver the scheduled reminders.
Does StreamDex collect or share my personal data?
Your personal library and viewing activity are stored locally and are not uploaded to a StreamDex account or server. The app does make requests to TMDB for catalogue information, and advertising services may process technical data when ads are shown. Read the privacy information for the full explanation.
Where can I get help or send feedback?
For questions, bug reports or suggestions, email streamdex@akissame.com.co. You can also leave feedback through the app's store listing.

Privacy, without the fine print.

The essentials about information stored on your device and the external services StreamDex uses. Expand a topic to read the details.

Last updated: November 2026
Data stored on your device

StreamDex works without an account or a server operated by us. Your watch history, ratings, achievements, XP, statistics and app preferences are stored locally on your device using the Isar database. We do not upload this personal library and profile information to a StreamDex account or server.

Third-party services

StreamDex uses the The Movie Database (TMDB) API to retrieve catalogue information such as titles, images, cast and streaming availability. Requests are sent to TMDB when you browse or search. This use is governed by TMDB's privacy policy. StreamDex is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.

When advertising is enabled, Google AdMob may process limited technical information to provide ads, under its own privacy policy. Google Play and Apple's App Store also handle app distribution under their respective policies.

Notifications

Premiere and new-episode reminders are generated and scheduled locally on your device. StreamDex uses refreshed release information to schedule them; notification content and your personal profile are not sent through a StreamDex notification server.

Profile export and sharing

You can choose to export your profile as a .sdx file containing information such as your library, statistics and achievements. You decide when and where to share that file. StreamDex does not automatically upload exported profiles, and we do not receive a copy when you share one through another app.

App permissions
  • Notifications: to alert you about premieres and new episodes.
  • Photos or media access: for image-related actions you choose to perform, where supported by your device.
  • Camera: only for optional features such as setting a custom profile photo, where available.

Permissions are used to provide the corresponding features; they are not a mechanism for uploading your personal library to StreamDex servers.

Children's privacy

StreamDex does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Core profile and viewing data are stored locally. Third-party services, including catalogue and advertising providers, remain subject to their own policies and age-related requirements.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as StreamDex evolves. Any changes will be reflected on this page together with a revised update date.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact streamdex@akissame.com.co.
