Frequently asked questions

Everything you wanted to know.

Is it really completely free?
Yes, no asterisks. StreamDex is free forever. There's no Premium plan, no subscription, no locked features.
Do I need to create an account?
No. Everything runs locally on your device. No sign-up, no email, no password. Just install and start.
How does the XP system work?
You earn XP by marking movies as watched, completing seasons, logging rewatches and unlocking achievements. XP raises your level and unlocks new ranks.
Can I transfer my data to a new phone?
Yes. Export your profile as a .sdx file from your profile and import it on your new device. No cloud, no server.
Do notifications work without internet?
Yes. Premiere and new-episode notifications are 100% local. Data refreshes whenever you open the app with a connection.
What data does the app collect?
None. Your watch history, statistics and achievements live only on your device. We don't send anything to any server.
Is it available on iOS?
StreamDex is currently on Android. The iOS version is in development and coming soon to the App Store.
How do I report a bug or suggest a feature?
You can email us at streamdex@akissame.com.co or leave a review on Google Play. We read all feedback.

Privacy Policy

How StreamDex handles your data — in short: it mostly doesn't, because it doesn't need to.

Last updated: January 2026

Data we collect

StreamDex is designed to work without accounts or servers of our own. Your watch history, ratings, achievements, XP, statistics and app preferences are stored locally on your device using an on-device database (Isar). We do not collect, transmit or store this information on any remote server operated by us.

Third-party services

To provide movie and show information, StreamDex uses The Movie Database (TMDB) API. When you browse or search content, requests are sent to TMDB to retrieve titles, images, cast and availability data. This use is governed by TMDB's own privacy policy. StreamDex is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.

If enabled, the app may also use Google AdMob to display ads and Google Play services for distribution. These providers may collect limited technical data (such as device identifiers) under their own privacy policies, independent of StreamDex.

Notifications

Push notifications for upcoming premieres and new episodes are generated and scheduled entirely on your device. No notification content or personal data is sent through an external server.

Profile export & sharing

You may choose to export your profile as a .sdx file containing your library, stats and achievements. This file is created and shared directly by you (e.g. via messaging apps) — StreamDex never uploads it automatically, and we have no access to files you export or import.

Permissions

  • Notifications — to alert you about premieres and new episodes.
  • Storage / Photos — to save or share images from the app.
  • Camera — only if you choose to set a custom profile photo.

None of these permissions are used to collect or transmit personal data off your device.

Children's privacy

StreamDex does not knowingly collect personal information from children, as the app does not collect personal information from anyone — all data stays on-device.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "last updated" date.

Contact
